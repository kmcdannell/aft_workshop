What to measure

  • Measure only confined tracks: etched tracks lying wholly inside the grain, parallel to the polished surface.
  • Measure tip to tip. Decide for yourself which tracks are good enough to measure.
  • Things to think about: how the etchant reached the track, whether both ends are sharp and in focus, and whether the track is fully etched.
