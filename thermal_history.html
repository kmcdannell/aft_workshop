Reading the data

  • Long, narrow track lengths mean the sample cooled quickly through the partial annealing zone (shaded orange).
  • Short tracks mean it spent a long time in that zone, or was reheated into it.
  • The age depends mostly on when the sample last cooled through about 100–110 °C, and on how long it stayed in the zone afterwards.
  • Temperatures above about 120 °C erase all tracks, so the data say little about the path while it was that hot.
